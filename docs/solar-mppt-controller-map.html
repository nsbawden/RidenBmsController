Power tier (inside Tracking)

Gates shade logic vs high-sun hunt

Low power

Pout < 70 W for 20 s of tracking ticks only (recovery excluded). Enables SH band, Pin decline, soft VTune, shade-collapse knee lower. No fast acquire.

High power

Pout ≥ 80 W for 5 s of tracking ticks. Classic FI–HU hunt only. Fast acquire and knee-delay ladder active.

Hysteresis band 70–80 W holds current tier. Mode sampling pauses during recovery cycles.

VTune / probe subsystem

Mostly hidden from Dashboard — see Tools → Events

Probe pacing (vtuneProbePhase)

PhaseMeaning
--Idle — normal maintenance delay between down-probes
WaitLockAfter down-probe — wait for FI hunt lock (3 ticks)
PauseHunt locked — soak before power check or next probe
BlockedPower dropped after probe — VTune descent blocked, reverted

Related flags

  • kneeProbeFast — rapid down-probes after N stable maintenance downshifts (high power only)
  • effectiveKneeDelaySeconds — minutes between maintenance probes; climbs after clean periods, snaps to min on unscheduled collapse
  • probe-refuzz session — probe-caused collapse; knee delay unchanged; ends after stable FI hold
  • vtuneDescentBlocked — Pin dropped after probe; last step reverted
Recovery (collapse)

Recover tile + recoveryCycleCount

  1. Triggered when Vin falls below knee collapse threshold during tracking
  2. Drop ISET (severe → MIN_ISET)
  3. Knee action — raise (default) or soft-lower if low power + Pin declining (shade)
  4. PHASE_WAIT_VIN until Vin near knee again
  5. Ramp ISET back toward last working value

Unscheduled collapse resets knee delay to min. Probe-caused collapse may start refuzz session instead.
